PROJECT BACKGROUND

Enhancing Gelatin Heating Through Automated Temperature Control

The gelatin heating process in storage tanks requires stable and controlled temperature conditions to maintain material quality and ensure consistent production operations. This project focuses on implementing an automated heater control panel integrated with temperature sensing and control components to regulate the heating process. The system automatically manages heater operation based on the detected tank temperature, helping maintain the required heating conditions. Through this improvement, the heating process becomes more consistent and reliable, reducing the need for manual heater intervention while supporting operational efficiency and better process control.

02. ENGINEERING APPROACH

Challenges & Solutions

Implementing an automated temperature control system to improve heating consistency, regulate heater operation, and ensure reliable gelatin processing within the storage tank.

01 CHALLENGE

Inconsistent Heating Temperature

Maintaining the required gelatin temperature during the heating process can be challenging without an automatic temperature regulation system. Manual heater operation may lead to temperature fluctuations, inconsistent heating conditions, and increased operator intervention.

01 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Automated Temperature Regulation

Implement a PID-based temperature control system integrated with temperature sensors and a dedicated heater control panel. The controller continuously monitors the tank temperature and regulates heater operation based on the configured temperature setpoint to maintain consistent heating conditions.

02 CHALLENGE

Manual Heater Operation & Process Control

Without an integrated control panel, managing heater operation and monitoring temperature conditions can require additional manual intervention. This may reduce operational efficiency and make the heating process more difficult to control consistently.

02 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Integrated Heater Control Panel

Develop a dedicated electrical control panel that integrates the PID temperature controller, switching components, and necessary control devices. The panel provides centralized heater operation and automatic temperature-based control, improving operational convenience, process reliability, and overall heating management.

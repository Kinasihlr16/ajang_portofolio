PROJECT BACKGROUND

Enhancing Mixing Machine Protection Through Food-Grade Teflon Coating

Mixing machines used in food and pharmaceutical manufacturing require suitable product-contact surfaces to maintain hygiene, prevent contamination, and ensure reliable production operations. Direct contact between products and exposed mechanical surfaces may introduce contamination risks, while continuous friction can accelerate component wear and affect equipment performance. This project focuses on developing and applying food-grade Teflon lining to critical product-contact areas within the mixing system. The coating provides a protective surface designed to minimize direct contact with underlying mechanical components, reduce friction, and improve component durability. Production trials are conducted to evaluate the coating's impact on mixing performance and product quality. In addition, maintenance procedures are established to preserve coating condition and support long-term equipment reliability.

02. ENGINEERING APPROACH

Challenges & Solutions

Implementing food-grade Teflon coating to protect critical product-contact surfaces, minimize contamination risks, reduce mechanical friction,
and maintain reliable mixing performance in the production process.

01 CHALLENGE

Product Contamination & Surface Wear

Direct contact between exposed mechanical components and products may introduce contamination risks. Additionally, continuous friction during mixing operations can accelerate surface wear, reducing component durability and increasing maintenance requirements.

01 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Food-Grade Teflon Surface Protection

Develop and apply food-grade Teflon lining to critical product-contact surfaces, creating a protective barrier that helps minimize contamination risks, reduce friction, and improve component durability.

02 CHALLENGE

Mixing Performance & Process Reliability

Applying additional coating to machine contact surfaces must not negatively affect mixing performance, material handling, or product quality. The coating must also maintain its protective properties during continuous production operations.

02 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Performance Validation & Maintenance Improvement

Conduct production trials to evaluate coating performance, mixing consistency, and product quality. Establish improved maintenance procedures to preserve coating condition, maintain stable machine performance, and reduce long-term maintenance demands.

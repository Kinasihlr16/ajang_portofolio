PROJECT BACKGROUND

Enhancing Production Efficiency Through Integrated Bottle Coding and Labeling

In manufacturing environments, accurate product identification is essential for maintaining batch traceability, supporting quality control, and meeting product labeling requirements. Information such as batch numbers and expiry dates must be printed clearly and consistently on product labels. However, when coding and labeling are performed as separate operations, additional handling and production steps may be required, increasing process complexity and potentially affecting production efficiency. This project focuses on integrating a cartridge-based printing system into an existing bottle labeling machine to enable product information printing directly onto labels during the labeling process. The printing unit operates in coordination with bottle movement along the production conveyor, allowing coding activities to be performed within the existing production workflow. This integration aims to reduce separate processing steps, minimize manual handling, and improve consistency in product coding and traceability.

Challenges & Solutions

01 CHALLENGE

Coding Synchronization with Bottle Movement

Integrating a cartridge printer into an existing labeling machine requires accurate coordination between the printing operation and bottle movement along the conveyor. Improper timing may result in misaligned, incomplete, or inconsistent product coding.

01 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Integrated Cartridge Printing System

Integrate the cartridge-based printer into the bottle labeling machine and coordinate its printing operation with bottle movement to ensure accurate placement of batch numbers and expiry dates on product labels.

02 CHALLENGE

Coding Quality & Production Reliability

Maintaining clear and consistent printed information during continuous production operations can be challenging. Variations in printing position, timing, or machine operation may affect coding readability and production consistency.

02 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Printing Adjustment & Performance Validation

Adjust the printer positioning and printing parameters to achieve consistent coding placement and readability. Conduct production trials to evaluate printing quality, synchronization, and overall machine performance.

01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding the Project

Integrating machine operation and electrical energy data into a centralized monitoring system for improved operational visibility and engineering decision-making.

PROJECT BACKGROUND

Connecting Machine Operations with Energy Insights

Manufacturing equipment and HVAC systems generate valuable operational and electrical data that can help engineering teams understand equipment utilization, monitor energy consumption, and identify opportunities for operational improvement. This project focuses on developing an integrated IoT-based monitoring system that collects machine operating hours and electrical parameters, including voltage, current, power, power factor, and energy consumption. By integrating measurement devices, sensors, communication gateways, and a centralized dashboard, the system provides a more accessible view of machine performance and energy usage to support engineering analysis, energy management, and maintenance planning.

02. ENGINEERING APPROACH

Challenges & Solutions

Bridging machine operation data and electrical energy measurements through an integrated IoT monitoring approach for manufacturing environments.

01 CHALLENGE

Disconnected Machine & Energy Data

Machine operating hours and electrical measurements represent different aspects of equipment performance. Without a unified monitoring approach, understanding machine utilization alongside energy consumption can be challenging.

01 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Unified Monitoring Architecture

Integrate machine operating-hour tracking, electrical measurement devices, IoT communication gateways, and a centralized dashboard to bring operational and energy data into a consolidated monitoring environment.

02 CHALLENGE

Interpreting Electrical Performance

Raw electrical measurements, including voltage, current, power, power factor, and energy consumption, can be difficult to interpret collectively when assessing equipment usage and energy patterns.

02 ENGINEERING SOLUTION

Centralized Energy Visualization

Organize electrical parameters and energy consumption data into a structured dashboard that enables engineering teams to review measurements, observe usage patterns, and identify potential opportunities for operational improvement.
